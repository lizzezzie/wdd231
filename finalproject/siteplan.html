Personal Growth Knowledge Explorer - Site Plan

Site Name

Personal Growth Knowledge Explorer

This name reflects a platform designed to help users explore structured knowledge and guidance for improving their emotional wellbeing, personal growth, and physical health.

Site Purpose

The site provides users with structured insights and practical guidance to support personal development. It allows users to explore topics based on their needs, read meaningful advice, and apply actionable steps to improve their lives.

Scenarios

  • How can I manage stress and emotional overwhelm when everything feels like too much?
  • What practical steps can I take to build discipline and stay consistent in my personal growth?
  • How can I start improving my physical health without feeling discouraged or overwhelmed?

Color Scheme

  • Deep Blue (#1E3A8A) - Used for headings, navigation, and footer
  • Vibrant Teal (#14B8A6) - Used for accents and section titles
  • Warm Orange (#F97316) - Used for highlights and call-to-action elements
  • Light Gray (#F1F5F9) - Used for background

Typography

Font: Roboto (sans-serif)

Roboto will be used for both headings and body text to maintain a clean, modern, and readable design.
